Frequently Asked Questions

Is this game free to play?
Yes. Aladdin's Wishes is completely free. There are no in-app purchases, no subscription, and no real-money transactions of any kind. Ads are shown to support development.
Can I win real money?
No. This is a simulated casino game for entertainment only. Virtual coins have no monetary value and cannot be redeemed, exchanged, or transferred for real money or prizes.
My coin balance is gone after reinstalling. Can I recover it?
Game progress is stored locally on your device only and is not backed up to iCloud or any server. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes your saved data. We are unable to restore balances. This is by design to protect your privacy.
My coins are not the same on two devices. Why?
Progress is per-device and intentionally not synced across devices. Each device keeps its own independent save data.
The game is not loading or crashing. What should I do?
Try these steps in order:
  1. Force-close the app and reopen it.
  2. Restart your iPhone.
  3. Check that you have iOS 14.0 or later (Settings → General → About → Software Version).
  4. Delete the app and reinstall from the App Store.
If the issue persists, please email us with your device model and iOS version.
I'm seeing ads I don't want. Can I turn them off?
Ads support the free game. To reduce ad personalization, you can select "Ask App Not to Track" in the App Tracking Transparency prompt, or adjust your iOS privacy settings under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
The game doesn't work without internet. Is that expected?
Yes. Aladdin's Wishes is fully offline. Gameplay does not require an internet connection. Ads may not load without connectivity, but the game itself will run normally.
How do I change the game language?
The game follows your device language. Go to Settings → General → Language & Region → iPhone Language. Set to English or 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese) and relaunch the app.
How do I request deletion of my data?
The app stores no personal data on our servers. All game data is local to your device — deleting the app removes it completely. For ad-related data held by Google, visit myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.

About the App

Aladdin's Wishes is an offline, free-to-play simulated slot machine game. It contains no real-money gambling mechanics. The app is rated 17+ on the App Store and requires users to confirm they are at least 18 years old before playing.

常见问题

这款游戏是免费的吗？
是的，Aladdin's Wishes 完全免费。没有应用内购买、订阅或任何形式的真实货币交易。游戏通过广告获得支持。
我能赢取真实货币吗？
不能。本游戏为纯娱乐模拟赌场游戏。游戏内的虚拟金币没有任何货币价值，无法兑换、变现或转让为真实货币或奖品。
重新安装后金币余额消失了，能找回吗？
游戏进度仅保存在您的设备本地，不会备份至 iCloud 或任何服务器。卸载应用将永久删除存档数据，我们无法恢复余额。此设计旨在保护您的隐私。
两台设备上的金币数量不一样，为什么？
游戏进度以设备为单位独立保存，不会在设备间同步。每台设备均有各自独立的存档数据。
游戏无法加载或频繁崩溃，怎么办？
请依次尝试以下步骤：
  1. 强制关闭应用后重新打开。
  2. 重启 iPhone。
  3. 确认 iOS 版本为 14.0 或更高（设置 → 通用 → 关于本机 → 软件版本）。
  4. 删除应用后从 App Store 重新安装。
如问题持续，请发送邮件并注明设备型号及 iOS 版本，我们将为您协助排查。
我不想看到某些广告，能关闭吗？
广告是游戏免费运营的支持来源。如需减少个性化广告，您可在应用追踪透明度弹窗中选择"要求应用不要追踪"，或在 设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪 中调整相关权限。
没有网络游戏就无法运行，这正常吗？
是的，Aladdin's Wishes 是完全离线游戏。游戏玩法无需联网。无网络时广告可能无法加载，但游戏本身可正常运行。
如何切换游戏语言？
游戏语言跟随设备语言。前往 设置 → 通用 → 语言与地区 → iPhone 语言，设置为 English 或简体中文，重新启动应用后生效。
如何申请删除我的数据？
本应用不在我们的服务器上存储任何个人数据。所有游戏数据仅保存在您的设备本地——卸载应用即可彻底删除。如需处理 Google 广告相关数据，请访问 myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy。
