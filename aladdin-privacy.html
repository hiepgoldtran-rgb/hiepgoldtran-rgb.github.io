1. Overview

Aladdin's Wishes is a free, offline, simulated casino game intended for entertainment purposes only. The App does not involve real-money gambling, in-app purchases, deposits, withdrawals, or any cash-equivalent transactions. Virtual coins shown inside the App have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for real currency or prizes. The App is intended for users aged 17 and over.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information the App itself collects

The App does not require account registration, sign-in, or personal information. We do not collect, store on our servers, or transmit any of the following: name, email, phone number, address, payment details, contacts, photos, microphone audio, or precise location.

Game progress (virtual coin balance, settings, language preference, age-gate acknowledgement) is stored only on your device using local storage. This data never leaves the device and is excluded from iCloud backup. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes this data.

2.2 Information collected by third-party advertising services

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process the following information to serve ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS), if you have permitted tracking
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language, time zone, screen size)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it (country / region level)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, ad load events)
  • App identifier and version

Data collection by AdMob is governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. AdMob's specific data disclosures: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

We have configured AdMob to block ad categories related to gambling, casino, lottery, and online dating within this App.

3. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS 14.5 and later, the App displays the Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt before any tracking activity occurs. You may choose Allow or Ask App Not to Track. If you choose to ask the App not to track, the IDFA is not available to AdMob, and only non-personalized ads will be shown.

4. GDPR consent (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, the App displays a consent form provided by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before serving ads. You may grant or refuse consent to each advertising purpose. Refusing consent means only non-personalized ads will be shown; the App will continue to function normally. You can change your choice at any time within the App's settings.

Legal bases under GDPR:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)) — for personalized advertising and related tracking.
  • Legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) — for fraud prevention, security, and non-personalized ad delivery.

Your GDPR rights include: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise these rights with respect to the App, contact hiepgoldtran@gmail.com. For data held by Google, please contact Google directly via the links above.

5. California (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. Sharing of advertising identifiers with Google AdMob for cross-context behavioral advertising may qualify as "sharing" under the CPRA. California residents may opt out by selecting "Ask App Not to Track" in the iOS ATT prompt, or by adjusting the in-App consent settings if available.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children. Per Apple's age-rating questionnaire, the App is rated 17+ and the App contains an age gate requiring the user to confirm they are at least 18 years old before play begins. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used the App, please contact us and we will assist as appropriate.

7. Data retention

Local game data persists on the device until you uninstall the App or clear App storage. We do not maintain user data on our own servers. Data retained by Google AdMob is governed by Google's retention policies.

8. Data security

Because the App operates offline and stores data only on the device, the security of that data depends on the security of your device. We recommend using a device passcode and keeping your operating system up to date. For data transmitted to AdMob, transport security is provided by HTTPS as implemented by Google's SDK.

9. International transfers

Data collected by Google AdMob may be processed in the United States and other jurisdictions where Google operates. Google relies on Standard Contractual Clauses and other mechanisms for international transfers, as described in Google's privacy policy.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective date" at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be announced via an in-App notice or App Store release notes.
